TL;DR

27 percent of the improvements highlighted across 50 recent VLA papers are smaller than the noise floor of the protocol that produced them. Rerunning the identical standard LIBERO evaluation with nothing changed but the flow-sampling seed moves the headline number by 2.2 points (86 recomputable comparisons audited); on informative mid-difficulty tasks the swing is 7.5 points, the size of the field's median claimed gap. Two of the three inference knobs everyone sets by habit are set wrong, and the third is not a knob at all.

Findings

  • The default is dominated. For pi0.5, a single Euler step at horizon 10 matches the 10-step default at a third of the control latency: statistically indistinguishable by a pre-declared equivalence standard, on the unmodified public checkpoint, no distillation. Its one measured price is about 4 points under camera-viewpoint shift (confirmed by a pre-declared 838-pair sample: 4.7 points, exact McNemar p=0.0008).
  • Everyone tunes the wrong dial. Execution horizon dominates denoising steps: replanning every step costs 9 to 41 points across all three policies (a truncated interim look had suggested SmolVLA collapses; the pre-declared completion to full n showed a task-composition artifact). Three for three across two model families.
  • The seed is not a dial. With the initial state fixed, flow-sampling noise carries 84, 81, and 61 percent of outcome variance across the three policies, and the decomposition structure replicates across three environment seeds while the headline level moves. Averaging over sampling seeds is a property of the flow-policy class, not a nicety for one model.
  • Rankings survive off the floor; margins never do. The same 16.5-point clean gap stands for anything from 9.6 to 67 points depending on the perturbation axis, and the one significant rank inversion (SmolVLA over pi0, exact p=0.0022) appears exactly where an axis floors the trailing policies. Leaderboard order is trustworthy off the floor; leaderboard distance carries almost no information.
  • The field's protocols cannot see their own claims. At the audited median of 50 rollouts per task the minimum detectable effect is 18 points, over a third of highlighted comparisons state no usable rollout count, and nearly a third of the recomputable ones fail an exact test at their own n.

Tooling

Every number regenerates from released per-episode JSONL records by one script, including the tables and the abstract's numerals. The harness is pip-installable (pip install roborigor): exact intervals, paired comparisons, a rollout-budget calculator, variance decomposition, and a campaign runner with exactly-once resume.
